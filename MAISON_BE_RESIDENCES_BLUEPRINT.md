# MAISON BE RESIDENCES — Website Blueprint & AI Design Brief

---

## 1. Project Overview & Strategy

* **Brand Name:** Maison Be Residences
* **Market:** Nigeria (High-Net-Worth Individuals, Luxury Homeowners & Global Diaspora Investors)
* **Design Benchmark & Inspiration:** High-converting luxury real estate developers (**DAMAC, Emaar, Aldar, Danube**)
* **Brand Identity & Aesthetic:**
  * **Primary Palette:** Deep Midnight / Royal Navy Blue (`#0B1325`, `#0D1B2A`), Pure Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`), Soft Warm Greys (`#F8F9FA`)
  * **Accent Color:** Champagne Gold / Warm Metallic Brass (`#D4AF37`, `#C5A059`, `#E5C378`)
  * **Typography Style:** High-end Editorial Serif (e.g., *Cormorant Garamond*, *Playfair Display*) paired with clean Modern Sans-Serif (e.g., *Plus Jakarta Sans*, *Inter*)
  * **Tone of Voice:** Prestigious, Exclusive, Architecturally Refined, High-Yield Investment

---

## 2. Recommended Website Structure

### Option A: High-Converting Project Microsite (Recommended for Lead Generation Pitch)
A seamless, single-page immersive landing page equipped with sticky conversion points (WhatsApp chat, brochure download modal, private tour booking).

### Option B: Full Multi-Page Property Portal (Phase 2)
1. **Home / Project Showcase**
2. **Residences & Floor Plans** (Unit breakdowns, 3D tours, specifications)
3. **Amenities & Lifestyle** (Wellness, leisure, concierge services)
4. **Location & Connectivity** (Neighbourhood guide, travel times)
5. **Investment & Payment Plans** (ROI calculations, payment schedules)
6. **About Developer / Maison Be** (Legacy, craftsmanship, credentials)
7. **Contact / VIP Private Viewing**
